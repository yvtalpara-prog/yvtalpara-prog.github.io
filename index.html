SWAGGERZ Backup

This app is used only by SWAGGERZ CLUB, Rajkot, to copy its own encrypted nightly database backup into the owner's Google Drive.

Privacy policy

The app uses the Google Drive "drive.file" permission. It can only create and read the backup files it uploads itself. It does not read, share or sell any other data in your Drive, and no data is given to anyone else.
